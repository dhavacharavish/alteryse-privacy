Privacy Policy — Alteryse Files

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · App: Alteryse Files (com.alteryse.filemanager)

Summary

Alteryse Files works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data with us or any third party. The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections of any kind.

What the app does with your data

  • Folders you choose. You grant the app access to one or more folders using Android's Storage Access Framework. The app reads the files in those folders (images, PDFs, and text files) only to build a searchable index on your device.
  • On-device text recognition and labeling. Optical character recognition (OCR, including Thai and English) and image content labeling are performed locally using software bundled in the app. Your images, documents, and the recognized text are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • Local search index. The extracted text and labels are stored in a private database in the app's own storage on your device, so you can search inside your files. This data never leaves your device.

Data collection and sharing

  • We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • The app requests no internet access, so it is technically unable to transmit your data.

Permissions the app uses, and why

  • Folder access (Storage Access Framework). To read and index the folders you explicitly choose. No broad storage permission is requested, and the app can only see folders you grant.
  • Foreground service (data sync) + notifications. To run indexing reliably in the background and show its progress. Indexing is on-device processing only.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, microphone, camera, phone, SMS, or broad media permissions.

Data storage and deletion

  • All app data (the search index) is stored in the app's private on-device storage.
  • You can remove any indexed folder from within the app, which drops its files from the index.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes the index and all data the app created.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
